What good analyst decks usually include

  1. 1. Executive summary first. Open with the change, the reason, and why stakeholders should care.
  2. 2. Metrics with interpretation. Numbers land better when the slide states the driver or implication explicitly.
  3. 3. Segment or deep-dive logic. Keep the analysis layered so the audience can move from summary to cause.
  4. 4. End on action. Recommendations and next steps turn analysis into decision support.

What to include in the brief

Audience + decision

Execs, product, ops, finance, or functional leads — and what they need to decide.

Top metrics

Current value, prior value, target, and time window for each KPI.

Drivers + segments

What caused the movement and which customer, region, or channel slice matters most.

Recommended actions

What the analysis suggests the team should change, test, or approve next.
