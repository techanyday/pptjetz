What strong marketing decks usually include

  1. 1. Market and audience context. Start with what changed and who this plan is for.
  2. 2. Message and differentiation. Make the story visible before you jump into channels or budget.
  3. 3. Rollout and measurement. Show timeline, KPIs, and what the team will watch closely.
  4. 4. Risk and next steps. Keep dependencies, asks, and decisions obvious for stakeholders.

If your launch is heavily time-based, pair this with the timeline slides page.

What to put in the brief

Goal + audience

Awareness, pipeline, launch readiness, retention, or executive update — and who will see the deck.

Message pillars

The 3 to 5 claims you want the audience to remember after the presentation.

Channels + timeline

Email, paid, SEO, events, enablement, and the sequence of rollout.

Measurement plan

Baseline, targets, leading indicators, and how the team will know if the plan is working.
