Common Marketing Deck Use Cases

Marketing teams create decks constantly. Some are forward-looking (plans and strategy), and others are backward-looking (results and learnings). PPTJet can draft both types as long as you provide the goal and a few constraints.

  • Campaign plans: audience, channels, creative themes, and timeline.
  • Product launch decks: positioning, messaging, rollout plan, and enablement.
  • Performance updates: KPIs, insights, wins, and next experiments.
  • Brand narratives: category story, differentiators, and proof.
  • Stakeholder alignment: what you need from sales, product, or leadership.

A Strong Marketing Presentation Structure

The best marketing decks feel like a short narrative. They build context, reveal insights, and end with a plan. Use this structure as a starting point and adjust based on audience.

  1. Context: what changed in the market and why now.
  2. Audience: who we are targeting and what they care about.
  3. Positioning: category, message, and differentiation.
  4. Plan: channels, creative angles, timeline, and budget assumptions.
  5. Measurement: KPIs, dashboards, and reporting cadence.
  6. Risks: constraints, dependencies, and mitigations.
  7. Next steps: decisions needed and owners.
