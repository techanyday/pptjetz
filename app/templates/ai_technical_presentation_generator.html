What strong technical decks usually include

  1. 1. State the system context. Why this architecture or decision matters now, and for whom.
  2. 2. Show the structure visually. Use diagrams, timelines, and component breakdowns where a paragraph would slow everyone down.
  3. 3. Make trade-offs explicit. Performance, reliability, security, cost, or delivery risk should be visible on the slide.
  4. 4. End with a recommendation. Clarify the preferred option, its rationale, and the next engineering step.
