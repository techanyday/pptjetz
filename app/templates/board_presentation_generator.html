A board deck sequence that usually works

  1. 1. Lead with the summary. What happened, what changed, and what decision the board should focus on.
  2. 2. Show performance clearly. Keep KPIs and financial movement easy to scan and interpret.
  3. 3. Bring forward risk and trade-offs. A good board deck is honest about what could derail the plan.
  4. 4. End with the ask. Clarify the approvals, guidance, or escalations you need.
