A product deck sequence that usually works

  1. 1. Start with the why now. Market signal, customer problem, or business constraint that makes the deck urgent.
  2. 2. Show the plan clearly. Themes, roadmap, milestones, dependencies, and trade-offs should each have a job.
  3. 3. Put metrics and risks on slides that are easy to scan. Use structured layouts instead of long bullet walls.
  4. 4. End with the decision. Make the ask obvious for leadership, engineering, or GTM partners.

What to include in the prompt

Audience + meeting type

Exec review, product sync, roadmap planning, launch go/no-go, or board update.

Goal + decision needed

Alignment, approval, prioritization, resourcing, or launch decision.

Must-include facts

Metrics, milestones, customer insights, dependencies, and top risks.

Constraints

Timeline, team capacity, compliance, and cross-functional dependencies.

Helpful follow-ups: KPI dashboards, risk matrices, and timeline slides.
