Online AI Slides vs Manual Slide Building

Manual slide building is slow because you solve content and design at the same time. Online generation helps when it drafts a structure you can react to. You can cut slides, tighten titles, and add real evidence quickly because the baseline is already there.

For best results, treat the generated deck like an outline. After exporting, rewrite slide titles to be point-first and add one concrete data point or example per key slide. That step is what makes the deck feel credible.

What to Do After Export (So the Deck Looks Real)

Treat the generated deck like a draft. The online workflow is fastest when you do the final pass inside PowerPoint. A small checklist is usually enough to make the slides feel intentional.

  • Rewrite slide titles to state conclusions, not topics.
  • Reduce bullets to the minimum needed to support the title.
  • Add one proof item to key slides (metric, screenshot, example).
  • Apply your theme (fonts, colors, logo) and align spacing.
  • Make next steps explicit with owners and dates.
