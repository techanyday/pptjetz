What strong training decks usually include

  1. 1. Start with the objective. Make it obvious what learners should know or be able to do afterward.
  2. 2. Teach in stages. Break the process into simple steps instead of letting one slide do everything.
  3. 3. Add examples and practice. Real screenshots and mini exercises make the deck more useful than theory alone.
  4. 4. End with support. Give people a checklist, resources, and where to go when they get stuck.
