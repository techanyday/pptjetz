What strong status update decks usually show

  1. 1. The delta. What changed since the last update should be obvious on the first slide or two.
  2. 2. Progress vs plan. Keep milestones, timeline, and completion status easy to scan.
  3. 3. Risks and blockers. Show what needs attention, who owns it, and what mitigation is underway.
  4. 4. Decisions and next steps. Make the sponsor ask explicit so the meeting can move.
