A simple student deck structure that works

  1. 1. Open with the point. Say what your presentation is arguing or explaining, not just the topic name.
  2. 2. Keep the middle scannable. Definitions, evidence, examples, and comparisons should each have their own slide role.
  3. 3. Add proof. Quotes, data, references, and examples make the deck feel researched rather than generic.
  4. 4. End with takeaway + sources. Close with what the audience should remember and the references you used.

If your topic is chronological, the timeline slides page can help you build a cleaner sequence.
