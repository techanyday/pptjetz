How to Iterate (So Unlimited Drafts Actually Help)

Unlimited generation is most useful when you change one thing at a time. Instead of pasting more text, adjust a single constraint and compare the drafts. This makes it easy to converge on a clear structure.

  • Change slide count: try 8 slides vs 12 slides.
  • Change the goal: “inform” vs “recommend” produces a different structure.
  • Add must-include sections: KPIs, timeline, risks, next steps.
  • Specify the audience level: executives want less detail and clearer conclusions.

After you pick the best storyline, export and do the polish pass once. Iteration is for structure. PowerPoint is for final design.
