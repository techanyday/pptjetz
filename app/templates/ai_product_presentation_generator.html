What strong product decks usually show

  1. 1. Why this matters now. Context from customers, market signals, or internal constraints.
  2. 2. What the team learned. Data, feedback, or discovery inputs that justify the plan.
  3. 3. How the plan unfolds. Roadmaps, timelines, and milestones help the room see the logic.
  4. 4. What decision is needed. Approval, prioritization, scope, or resource alignment should be explicit.
