Tighten the Draft (Make It Sound Less Generic)

The fastest improvement is a “tightening” pass. Keep the structure, but rewrite a few key elements so the deck sounds like it was written for your audience. This is where the slides become presentation-ready.

  • Make titles specific: replace “Challenges” with a claim like “Churn is driven by onboarding friction.”
  • Replace placeholders: use your product name, segment, and timeline.
  • Limit bullets: 3–5 bullets max per slide for scannability.
  • Add one proof element to the key slides: a metric, screenshot, or example.
