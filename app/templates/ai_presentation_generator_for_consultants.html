A consulting deck workflow that usually works

  1. 1. Define the decision. State what the client needs to approve, align on, or understand by the end.
  2. 2. Summarize the evidence. Use 5 to 10 bullets with findings, metrics, and constraints instead of long paragraphs.
  3. 3. Ask for the right slide types. Roadmap, KPI, risk, comparison, and next-step slides make strategy decks easier to scan.
  4. 4. Polish for the room. Tighten titles, add one proof point per key claim, and apply the client template after export.
