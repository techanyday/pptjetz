A Simple Input Template That Produces Better Slideshows

Most “AI slideshow” disappointments come from unclear input. If you paste a vague topic, you’ll get vague slides. If you provide a tight brief, you’ll get a draft that feels like a real deck. Use the template below, then generate multiple drafts and choose the cleanest storyline.

Audience: [who will watch/read the slideshow]

Goal: [inform / persuade / align / decide]

Time limit: [5 min / 10 min / 20 min]

Slide count: [8–14]

Sections: [context, key message, proof, options, recommendation, next steps]

Must include: [metrics, timeline, risks, owners, decision needed]

Notes/bullets: [6–12 bullets with real nouns and numbers]

If your starting point is long paragraphs, consider drafting bullets first using convert text to PowerPoint. Your slideshow will be clearer when each slide supports one takeaway.

Quality Checklist (What to Fix After Export)

Think of the export as a draft you can improve quickly. A ten-minute cleanup pass is usually enough to make an AI-generated slideshow feel intentional. Focus on clarity first, then design.

  • Titles: rewrite each title so it states a conclusion, not a topic.
  • Bullets: cut any bullet that repeats the title or says “in order to.”
  • Evidence: add one metric, screenshot, quote, or example to the key slides.
  • One idea per slide: split slides that mix multiple messages.
  • Last slide: make the next step explicit (owner, date, decision required).

Slide Types That Make a Slideshow Feel Professional

A slideshow is easier to follow when you mix a few clear slide types. Instead of repeating “title + bullets” for every slide, include 2–3 structured slides that help your audience scan quickly. When you generate with PPTJet, you can ask for these slide types explicitly.

  • Agenda + recap to make the story predictable.
  • Comparison (option A vs option B) when you need a decision.
  • Timeline / roadmap when sequencing matters.
  • KPI snapshot when you need credibility fast.
  • Risks + mitigations to show you’ve thought ahead.

For business-style slideshow structures, you can also generate dedicated slides like KPI dashboards or a risk matrix and copy them into your final deck.
