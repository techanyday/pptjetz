How to Use PPTJet (Fast Workflow)

The easiest way to get strong results is to give the generator a clear goal and a few constraints. You don’t need a long prompt. A short, structured input creates better slides than a paragraph of text.

  1. Write the topic and the audience (team update, class, client, investors).
  2. Add 5–10 bullets with key points or sections you want included.
  3. Set constraints: slide count, tone (simple, executive, classroom), and any must-include sections.
  4. Generate and export to PowerPoint, then polish headings and visuals.

How to Make the Output Look Professional

AI gives you speed, but polish still matters. After export, a 5-minute clean-up pass can dramatically improve the final deck.

  • Rewrite titles as conclusions: make headings say the point, not the topic.
  • Reduce text: one slide should communicate one idea.
  • Add evidence: one chart, screenshot, or example per key claim.
  • Keep spacing consistent: align text boxes and use one font size range.
