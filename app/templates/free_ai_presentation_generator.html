How to get a stronger deck draft

  1. 1. State the audience and decision. Say who the deck is for and what should happen after they see it.
  2. 2. Add 5 to 10 concrete bullets. Short, structured inputs outperform long vague paragraphs.
  3. 3. Set constraints. Slide count, tone, must-include sections, KPIs, risks, next steps.
  4. 4. Export and polish. Keep the structure, then tighten titles and swap in proof.
