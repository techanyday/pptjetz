A sales deck flow that is easier to follow

  1. 1. State the problem clearly. The buyer should see themselves in the opening slides.
  2. 2. Show the impact. Explain the operational, revenue, or risk consequence of staying where they are.
  3. 3. Support the claim with proof. Results, quotes, metrics, and examples make the deck credible.
  4. 4. End on next steps. Give the rep and the buyer a clear path after the meeting.

What to include in the prompt

Buyer profile

Top pains + outcome

Differentiators + proof

Why you win, which proof points matter, and what objections you need to address.

Next-step motion

Pilot, pricing review, technical validation, legal step, or implementation plan.
