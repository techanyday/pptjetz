A Practical Workflow for Making Presentations with AI

The fastest path to a professional deck is a repeatable workflow. Here’s a simple approach that works for business, school, and training decks.

  1. Define the audience and decision. Are you informing, persuading, or teaching?
  2. Write the one-sentence takeaway you want remembered.
  3. Provide a short brief with key points and constraints (slide count, tone, sections).
  4. Generate the first draft and check the slide order for logic.
  5. Export the .pptx and apply branding, examples, and visuals.

Quality Safeguards for Professional Slides

A professional deck feels consistent. Use these safeguards to keep your output clean.

  • Limit slide count to keep the deck tight.
  • Use one message per slide (headline states the conclusion).
  • Prefer bullets over paragraphs for readability.
  • Use visual slide types when they help (timeline for sequence, KPI for metrics, risk matrix for uncertainty).
  • Polish in PowerPoint: align, simplify, and apply theme.
