Frequently Asked Questions

What is an investor pitch deck?

An investor pitch deck is a short presentation that explains your company, the market opportunity, your product, traction, and the funding ask. It’s the deck you share with angels or venture capital teams to earn a meeting and move the process forward.

What slides should be included in an investor pitch deck?

Most investor decks cover the problem, solution, market size, business model, competition, go-to-market, traction, financials, and the ask. PPTJet generates that core structure so you can refine the content with your real data.

Can I edit the generated pitch deck?

Yes. You export the deck and edit it in PowerPoint like a normal presentation—swap screenshots, adjust numbers, rewrite phrasing, and add your branding.

Is this suitable for seed or Series A fundraising?

Yes. The structure works well for seed round fundraising and can also support a more detailed series A pitch deck. The key difference is depth: later stages typically require clearer metrics, tighter positioning, and stronger proof, which you can add after export.
