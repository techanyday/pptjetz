Building an investor pitch deck often starts with a blank slide: deciding what belongs on each page, how to sequence the story, and how to make it look clean under time pressure. When this pitch deck generator is useful

  • Preparing a startup pitch for angel investors or venture capital meetings
  • Turning a business idea into a clear narrative (problem, solution, traction, and the ask)
  • Creating a consistent slide structure before you refine numbers, visuals, and wording

Visual, slide-ready elements (not just text)
