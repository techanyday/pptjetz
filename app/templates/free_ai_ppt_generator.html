What to Input to Generate a Better PPT

The best inputs are short and structured. You’re telling the generator what to emphasize, not asking it to guess.

  1. Define audience: customer, internal leadership, class, or training group.
  2. Define outcome: approve a plan, understand progress, learn a process, or decide next steps.
  3. List key points: 4–8 bullets, each a real claim or fact.
  4. Add a slide list if you need it (agenda, timeline, risks, KPIs, next steps).

How to Polish the AI Draft in PowerPoint

Treat the generated PPT as a starting point. Professional decks are won in the edit.

  • Apply a theme (fonts, color palette, layout grid).
  • Replace placeholders with your numbers, screenshots, and examples.
  • Shorten slide text until each slide can be understood quickly.
  • Check narrative flow: each slide should set up the next.
