A classroom-friendly slide rhythm

  1. 1. Open with the objective. Make the learning goal visible in student-friendly language.
  2. 2. Break concepts into stages. Definition, example, guided practice, and recap should each have a clear slide role.
  3. 3. Add checks for understanding. Build in quick prompts, questions, or an exit ticket so the deck supports teaching, not just talking.
  4. 4. Keep it editable. After export, adjust phrasing, examples, and accessibility details for your specific class.
