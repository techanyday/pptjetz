Slide Types to Request (So the Deck Isn’t Repetitive)

If every slide is “title + bullets,” the deck can feel repetitive. Ask for 2–3 structured slide types that match your use case.

  • Timeline / roadmap when sequencing matters.
  • KPI snapshot when you need credibility quickly.
  • Options + trade-offs when a decision is required.
  • Risks + mitigations when you need realism.

You can generate dedicated slides like KPI dashboard slides and drop them into the exported .pptx.

One-Minute Final Check Before You Present

Before presenting, scan the deck for three issues: titles that don’t state a point, slides that contain more than one idea, and a missing closing slide. Fix those three things and the draft will feel significantly more polished without adding extra content.
