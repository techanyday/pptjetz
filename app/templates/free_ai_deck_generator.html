Final Pass: Make the Deck Sound Like You

After export, you can make the deck feel human and brand-consistent with a short “voice” pass. This step is what turns a good draft into a deck you can send to leadership or a customer.

  • Replace generic words with your terms (product names, team names, customer segments).
  • Add specifics where the audience expects them (KPIs, dates, costs, owner names).
  • Keep slides scannable: 3–5 bullets max per slide.
  • Use consistent structure: problem → proof → implication → next step.

How to Edit a Generated Deck in 10 Minutes

You don’t need a full redesign to make a generated deck useful. A quick edit pass focused on clarity and credibility usually gets you 80% of the way there. Use this checklist right after you export the .pptx.

  1. Cut filler: remove slides that repeat the same idea with different words.
  2. Strengthen titles: change “Market Overview” to a claim like “Market is growing, but churn is the risk.”
  3. Add one proof slide: a chart, a customer quote, or a screenshot beats generic bullets.
  4. Clarify the ask: make the final slide a decision with owners and dates.

Need a specific slide type to drop into your deck? Generate a SWOT analysis or architecture diagram slide and place it where it supports the narrative.
