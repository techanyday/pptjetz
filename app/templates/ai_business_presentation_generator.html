What a strong business deck usually includes

  1. 1. Situation summary. Explain what changed, why it matters, and what leaders should focus on first.
  2. 2. Metrics and signal. Put the most decision-relevant numbers early and interpret them clearly.
  3. 3. Plan and risk. Use roadmaps, SWOT, or risk slides to make the trade-offs visible.
  4. 4. Recommendation and next steps. End the deck with an obvious ask and what happens after approval.
